THE PEOPLE OF PALAU REPRESENTED IN THE OLBIIL ERA KELULAU DO ENACT AS FOLLOWS:

#### 1 II Section 1. Legislative findings. The Olbiil Era Kelulau finds that the Republic's

2 II tax regime is outdated and in need of optimization. The Gross Revenue Tax (GRT) is 3 II notwell suited to the service-based high-value tourism economy the Republic is striving 4 II to build.

#### 5 II Accordingto the report ofthe Republic's 2007TaxReview TaskForce, the GRT

#### 6 II discourages economic growth by disadvantaging new and small businesses. Becausethe

7 II GRT is assessed separately at every transaction level, it benefits large vertically-

#### 8 II integrated businesses over small businesses that buy from wholesalers. According to

#### 9 II the same 2007 Tax Review Task Force, the GRT also discourages economic growth by

10 II disadvantaging foreign investment. Because the GRT is incompatible with tax regimes 11 II in many of the Republic's major investment source countries, it can lead to double- 12 II taxation and disqualify investors from tax benefits in their home jurisdictions.

#### 13 II VAT has become the most common tax regime in the world; most countries

#### 14 II collect a VAT. More pointedly, VAT has been adopted by most of the Republic's

15 II economic peers, and by close trading partners, includingJapan, Korea, the Philippines, 16 II and Taiwan.

#### 17 II The transition from GRT to VAT has been an objective of the Republic for

#### 18 II many years, and the details have been the subject of healthy debate within several

#### 19 II Constitutional Governments. Senate Bill9-14, introduced during the 9th OEK, was the

#### 20 II most recent iteration of this tax reform package, and allowed the leaders of this

#### 21 II Republic an opportunity to exchange views and coordinate with international experts

22 II to increase understanding ofthe relevant issues. 23 II The Olbiil Era Kelulau finds that it is in the best interest of the Republic to

#### 24 II transition away from the GRT and adopt a VAT system known as the Palau Goods and

1 II Services Tax.
