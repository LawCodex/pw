## PART I: PRELIMINARY

1. Short Title - Digital Residency Program.

2. Authorization 1) RPPL 11-14, the Digital Residency Act ("the Act"), amended Chapter 10 ofTitle 13 of the Palau National Code and added SubchapterIII, Digital Residency Programfor the Republic of Palau ("Palau"). The Act authorized the establishment of a Digital Residency Office (the

"Office") within the Ministry of Finance to oversee and manage all activities authorized, required or permitted by the Act, to includeall responsibilities ofservice providers. The Office is authorized to promulgate regulations under RPPL 11-14, Section 5. 2) Privacy Act, 6 PNCA §205 - 206 "Storage ofand access to personal information" require that an agency that maintains personal information shall ensure that the information is protected from loss, unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or other misuse. An agency that maintains personal information may not keep that information for longer than is required for the purposes for which the information may lawfully be used. An agency that holds personal information that was obtainedinconnection with one purpose shall not use the information for any other purpose, unless: the source of the personal information is publicly available; the individual concerned consents to the other purpose; use for another purpose is necessary to avoid prejudice to the maintenance or enforcement ofthe law by any agency in the prevention, detection, investigation, prosecution, and punishment of civil, administrative or criminal offenses; the use of the information for another purpose is necessary to prevent or lessen a serious threat to public health or public safety; the purpose for which the information is used is directly related to the purpose in connection with which the information was obtained; the purpose for which the information will be used will be for statistical or research purposes and will not be published, and could not reasonably be used to identify the individual concerned; or pursuant to the order ofa court ofcompetentjurisdiction.

3. Application-This regulation establishes cybersecurity policiesand proceduresin accordance with the provisions of International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 27001, Cyber Security Compliance. This regulation applies to the scope and responsibility of the Digital Residency Office in overseeing all payments made to the Republic of Palau, managing the contracted services of the Service Provider, and reviewing, storing and safeguarding all pending, approved and disapproved Digital Residency Applications.

## PART II: STATEMENT OF POLICY

1: 2: 3:

Purpose- This regulation is intended to ensure that the Office maintains a level of security, inabsolute terms and in relation to total assets, which (i) is adequate to protectthe interests of Digital Residency Program stakeholders, (ii) is commensurate with the legal requirements, contractual obligations and operational activities ofthe Digital Residency Program, and (iii) promotes public confidence in the Digital Residency Program, overall.

Scope - This regulation applies to the operation, maintenance and security ofthe Digital Residency Program of Palau and establishes the secure procedures for receiving, processing and storing Digital Residency Applications, sensitive correspondence, PII, and all other activities associated with operating the Office in Palau.

Responsibility - It is the responsibility ofthe Office director to establish and maintain a levelofsecuritythat complies at alltimes with therequirements ofRPPL 11-14,ISO 27001 and this regulation, and is fully adequate for the risk profile and activities ofthe Office.

The security levels required by this regulation are the minimum levels acceptable for designing, implementing and maintaining the cyber security of information management systems.

## PART III: IMPLEMENTATION AND SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS

1. Identification ofApplicable Legislation and Contractual Requirements. a. To avoid breaches of legal, statutory, regulatory or contractual requirements, all requirements should be identified and documented by the officeto ensure theirfulfillment.

Special attention is required when conducting online business or trading to take into account international agreements, industry standards, and best practices. b. The Office and all contracted vendors and service providers will establish and maintain procedures to identify, document and comply with all applicable statutory, regulatory and contractual requirements. c. The Office and all contracted vendors and service providers will establish and maintain documented controls to comply with all legal and contractual compliance requirements. d. Changes to requirements will be traceable to changes to implemented controls.

2. Information Security Roles and Responsibilities. a. The Office will designate, in writing, an Information Security Manager (ISM). b. The ISM appointment orders will designate reporting structure and responsibilities for information security. · c. The ISM, in coordination with the Information Systems Support Service (ISSS) ofthe Ministry ofFinance, will ensure compliance for all permanent and temporary employees, contractors, and third-party users. d. Segregation of duties is a traditional business control used to reduce vulnerability to human errorand deliberate misuse. The Officewill ensure independentverificationofdata and audit results. The Office will ensure that no one person is responsible for a majority of checks and balances. e. The Office will coordinate with the ISSS to ensure that critical activities are logged and the logs reviewed on a regular basis. The individuals whose activities are being reviewed will not be able to alter or delete these logs.

3. Information Security Awareness, Education and Training. a. All employees of the Office to include contractors must complete appropriate cyber security awareness education and training, annually, as relevant for theirjob function. b. All employees ofthe Office will be trained on the proper and safe use ofall information technology (IT) products and services required of their position, as well as the relevant security procedures. Refresher training should be conducted, annually. c. Every employee of the Office, to include contractors and third-party users, should be given a foundational level ofsecurity awareness training. d. Staffwith special responsibilities for security should, in addition to the Basic Security Awareness training, be provided with relevant, special training. . ·,

4. Information Security Management. a. The Office will ensure a consistent and effective approach to the management of information security incidents in collaborationwith the ISSS, including communicationon security events and vulnerabilities. b.The Office will coordinate with the ISSS to establish and maintain information security incident management procedures go handle and recover from system failures, errors, security breaches and more to include contingency arrangements and auditing activities.

5. Information Classification and Handling. a. The Office will ensure that all information processed and stored receives an appropriate level ofprotection in accordance with its importance to the organization. b. Information should be classified in terms of legal requirements, value, criticality and sensitivity to unauthorized disclosure or modification. c. PII is informationthat can be used to distinguish or trace an individual's identity, either alone or when combined with other information that is linked or linkable to a specific individual. Because there are many different types of information that can be used to distinguish or trace an individual's identity, the term PII is necessarily broad. d. It is important to recognize that information that is not PII can become PII whenever additional information is made publicly available, in any medium and from any source, that, when combinedwithotherinformationto identify a specific individual, could be used to identify an individual (e.g., Social Security Number (SSN), name, date ofbirth (DOB), home address, personal email). e. All breaches and compromises of PII must be reported, immediately to the ISM. A breach is the actual or suspected compromise, unauthorized disclosure, unauthorized acquisition, unauthorized access, and/or any similar occurrence where:

(1) A person other than an authorized user accesses or potentially accesses PII, or

(2) An authorized user accesses or potentially accesses PII for other than an authorized purpose.

6. System and Application Access Control. a. The Office will establish an access control policy to limit access to information and information processing facilities. b. The Office will prevent unauthorized access to systems and applications. Access to information provided by individual applications will be actively managed to match business and contractual requirements. Users will not be allowed access to application functions or data files without authorization by the information owner and without an established need to know or need to use. c. The Office will establish and enforce controls and accessibility audits through secure log-on procedures to access all Digital Residency Program information management systems. d.The Office will ensure all remote accessibility iscompletely disabled for all servers used as primary or back up file servers. e. Access control rights and rules will be clearly defined and will be clearly consistentwith the classification and handling requirements for the information. Any access to sensitive information will be granted or denied based on the "need to know" and "need to use" principles. f. The Office employees with access to sensitive or confidential information will be properly trained on information security and handling. g. Users will only be provided with access to the network and network services that they have been specifically authorized to use. h. Access to sensitive or confidential information will be consistent with legal, regulatory and contractual compliance requirements.

7. Information Transfer Policies and Procedures a. The Office will maintain the security ofinformation transferred within the organization and with any external entity, in order to prevent: 1) information being leaked ifsent to the wrong email address 2) information being intercepted by unauthorized people ifsent unprotected 3) information being compromised when left unattended in printers or fax machines. 4) staffbeing overheard discussing sensitive information in public. 5) messages and faxes being received in error due to mishandling. b. Information in electronic messaging will be appropriately protected. c. The Officewill regularly identify, document and reviewrequirements for confidentiality or non-disclosure agreements reflecting the organization's needs of the protection of information. c. Information will be protected from loss, unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or other misuse. d. Information will not be accessed or kept for longer than is required for the purposes for which the information may lawfully be used. e. Information obtained for Digital Residency Program purposes will not be used for any other purpose, unless: 1) the source of the personal information is publicly available; the individual concerned consents to the other purpose; 2) use for another purpose is necessary to avoid prejudice to the maintenance or enforcement of the law by any agency in the prevention, detection, investigation, prosecution, and punishment ofcivil, administrative or criminal offenses; 3) the use ofthe information for another purpose is necessary to prevent or lessen a serious threat to public health or public safety; 4) the purpose for which the information is used is directly related to the purpose in connection with which the information was obtained; 5) the purpose for which the information will be used will be for statistical or research purposes and will not be published, and could not reasonably be used to identify the individual concerned; 6) or pursuant to the order ofa court ofcompetentjurisdiction.

8. Asset Management and Handling. a. The Office will identify all organizational assets and an inventory ofthese assets shall be drawn up and maintained. An inventory ofall assets will be conducted monthly. b. The Office will assign ownership for all important assets and that each asset owner understands the tasks and duties for accountability ofthe assets. c. TheOffice will trackthe introductionofnewassets and disposal ofoldassets by updating the asset inventory, as appropriate. d. The Office will establish and enforce acceptable use controls to clearly describe the intended use oforganizational assets, and the boundaries for intended use. Controls will be established to detect misuse and non-compliance. All asset owners and users will be made aware ofthe acceptable use requirements and restrictions. e. The Office will ensure the return ofall organizational assets upon the termination ofan asset owner's employment, contract or agreement.

9. Physical and Environmental Security. a. The Office will prevent unauthorized physical access, damage and interference to the organization's information and information processing assets and facilities. b. The Office will define security perimeters and use them to protect areas that contain information sensitive or critical to the Digital Residency Program. c. The Office will maintain standard operating procedures for the physical access and protection of organizational assets used to receive, process and store Digital Residency Program information. These standard operating procedures will take into account environmental control requirements, measures to mitigate or prevent damage by fire and other hazards and monitoring requirements. d. Secure areas will be protected by appropriate entry controls to ensure that only authorized personnel are allowed access. e. Physical security for offices, room and facilities will be designed and applied to the Digital Residency Program spaces and operating areas consistent with the most sensitive and critical information handled or stored. Information security requirements must be identifiedand risk assessments conducted, risks mitigated, and residual risk acknowledged and accepted by the ISM. f. Physical protection against natural disaster, malicious attack or accidents shall be designed and applied. Measures will be put in place to prevent or mitigate the negative impacts of natural and technological disasters. The Office will incorporate system and facility resiliency to the greatest extent possible to ensure proper emergency support and environmental protections are in place.

10. Control ofOperational Software. a. The Office will coordinate with the ISSS to implement procedures for inventorying and controlling the installation and modification ofsoftware on operational systems used for the Digital Residency Program. Changes to operational software should only be made in the event ofvulnerability remediation and protection from malware, in the event ofnew business requirements, or as directed by the Minister ofFinance.

11. Technical Vulnerability Management. a. To prevent the exploitation oftechnical vulnerabilities, the Office will collaborate with the ISSS to obtaininformation about technical vulnerabilities ofinformation systems being used in a timely manner. The Digital Residency Program's risk exposure will be determined and the appropriate measures taken to eliminate or mitigate the associated residual risks. b. An assessmentofassociated risks for information systems used bythe Digital Residency Program will be conducted. Such measures can include the use ofautomated vulnerability scanning tools and manual penetration testing engagements for more sensitive environments, as deemed appropriate by the ISM. c. Assessment procedures will be clearly defined and repeatable. All tools will be configured to test all systems and notjust a sample. Reports from testing should be made available for audit, as well as reports from re-tests which verify that vulnerabilities identified in previous tests have been remediated.

12. Protection from Malware. a. The Office will coordinate with the ISSS to implement detection, prevention and recovery controls to ensure that information and information processing systems and facilities are protected against malware. b.Giventhe stand-alone nature ofcritical Digital Residency Program information systems, protectionsoftware and updates must be applied individually to each system. The ISM will coordinate with the ISSS to ensure anti-virus software is installed and maintained.

13. Information System Audit Controls. a. The Office will coordinate with the ISSS to minimize the impact ofaudit activities on operational systems by carefully planned and approved business processes and schedules. b. Any audit of information systems controls and tools will not compromise the information or operations being checked. c. All requirements mustbeidentified and appropriate authorizationexistsfrom operational management before executing any audit. Interruptions to business activities will be minimized. d. Audit results will be maintained by the ISM and the use ofany tools properly recorded.

Coordination for audits will be made with the ISSS to ensure that the audit personnel are independent ofthe Office and the activities being audited.

14. System Backup. a. To protect against data loss, backup copies ofDigital Residency Program information, software and system images will be produced in accordance with ISSS guidelines and schedules. b. Copies of all Digital Residency Applications transmitted to the Office by the Service Provider in compliance with the operating agreement will be stored as a backup copy to the files stored on the Service Provider's servers.

15. Logging and Monitoring. a. The Office will coordinate with the ISSS to ensure event logs recording user activities, exceptions, faults and information security events are produced, stored and regularly reviewed. b. Logging arrangements will be appropriate for the security requirements ofthe Office. c. At a minimum, log information will identify the event, the person causing the event, any changes made, the date and the time.

16. System Acquisition, Development and Maintenance. a. The Office will coordinate with the ISSS to ensure that information security-related requirements are included in the requirements for new information systems or enhancements to existing information systems. This ensures that information security remains an integral part ofthe information system lifecycle. b. Information involved in Digital Residency Application service transactions will be protectedto preventincompletetransmission, misrouting,unauthorized messagealteration, unauthorized disclosure, unauthorized message duplication or replay.

17. Compliance with Security Policies and Standards. a. The ISM in coordination with the ISSS will regularly review the compliance of information processing and procedures within the Digital Residency Program with the appropriate security policies, standards and any other security requirements. b. Information systems will undergo technical compliance reviews to ensure compliance

## PART IV: CORRECTIVE MEASURES

1: Remedial measures and sanctions- There will be a formal and communicated disciplinary process in place to take action against employees who have committed an information security breach, or any employee, permanent or temporary, contractor or associate who violates any provision ofthis regulation in a willful, negligent or flagrant manner which results, or is likely to result, inan unsafe or unsound condition for the Office orthat threatens the interests ofthe Digital Residency Program stakeholders.
